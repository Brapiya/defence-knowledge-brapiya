🎯 SSB Interview Complete Guide 🎯

Stage 1 Screening • Psychology • GTO • Personal Interview • OLQs

✨ Brapiya's SSB Success Mantra ✨

📌 Stage 1: Screening Test

  • OIR (Officer Intelligence Rating) - Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning
  • PPDT (Picture Perception & Discussion) - Write story with action + result
  • Brapiya Tip: Be confident, speak clearly, don't dominate

🧠 Stage 2: Psychology Test

  • TAT (Thematic Apperception Test) - 12 pictures, write positive stories
  • WAT (Word Association Test) - 60 words, first thought positive likho
  • SRT (Situation Reaction Test) - 60 situations, practical answers
  • SD (Self Description) - Parents, teachers, friends ke nazariye se

🏃 Stage 3: GTO Tasks

  • Group Discussion, Group Planning Exercise
  • Progressive Group Task (PGT), Half Group Task
  • Individual Obstacles, Lecturette (3 min speech)
  • Command Task - Leadership dikhao

🎙️ Stage 4: Personal Interview

  • Questions on bio-data, current affairs, defence knowledge
  • Why do you want to join defence forces?
  • Be honest, maintain eye contact, uniform pehen ke jao
